Detailed Description

Surface material definitions and presets.

Materials describe how light interacts with a surface:

  • Color (tint)
  • Reflection / refraction model
  • Roughness for glossy effects
  • Opacity for transparency
  • Index of refraction for refractive materials Includes the Material struct and a few predefined materials.
